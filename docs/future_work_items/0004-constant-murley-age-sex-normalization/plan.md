# Constant-Murley score: add the age/sex-normalized relative score

> **Status:** Future work item — not implemented, not approved.

## Gap

The IG captures the raw 0–100 Constant-Murley total and its 4 components (ADR-0090), not the
age/sex-normalized "relative CMS" (raw score ÷ an age-and-sex-matched healthy-reference value from
a published norm table) that the literature treats as more comparable across heterogeneous patient
populations.

## Why it matters

Cross-patient/cross-study comparability of the raw total is weaker than the normalized version; a
researcher pooling registry data with published cohorts would need to apply the normalization
externally.

## Note

Not requested by Hurley (names only "the Constant score", no normalization mechanism) or any
surgeon feedback point — a deliberate next-cycle addition, not a gap in what was scoped.
